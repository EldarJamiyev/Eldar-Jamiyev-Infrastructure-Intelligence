# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The EJII project welcomes participation that is respectful, professional, constructive, and focused on improving reliable infrastructure diagnostics and operations.

We expect everyone involved in project discussions, reviews, issues, and contributions to treat others fairly, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and keep technical criticism focused on the work.
- Support technical claims with evidence, reproducible observations, or clearly stated assumptions.
- Welcome constructive feedback and discuss disagreements professionally.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality; never publish passwords, tokens, private keys, personal information, customer data, or private infrastructure details.
- Keep discussions relevant, actionable, and considerate of other contributors' time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when this Code of Conduct is violated.

To report a conduct concern, contact the project owner privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/EldarJamiyev). Please include relevant context and avoid sharing sensitive information in a public issue.

Reports will be handled discreetly and reviewed with consideration for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.
